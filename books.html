Omnibus Collection • Volume I

The Lilith Chronicles Omnibus

Volume I: Awakening in the Shadows
Dark Fantasy Celestial Myth Angelic Order Reincarnation

Captured by ancient celestial powers and subjected to an ambitious attempt at rehabilitation through reincarnation into mortal lives, Lilith's journey begins in the shadows of divine architecture. As lives pass beyond counting, fragments of her true primordial self begin to stir.

In Volume I, witness the mortal awakenings and heavenly intrigues as ancient guardians attempt to restrain an immortal spirit that will not bend.

“In every mortal breath, a fragment of celestial flame refused to die.”

Omnibus Collection • Volume II

The Lilith Chronicles Omnibus

Volume II: Ascendance of the Queen Mother
Dragon Epic Goddess of Magic Queen Mother Dragon Cosmic Balance

Restored at last to her original form, Lilith rises as Guardian of Balance, Protector of Heaven, and Chief Angel. Yet her forgotten origins hold a power greater still: the Goddess of Magic, a legendary warrior, and a Dragon Changeling destined to ascend as Queen Mother Dragon.

Surrounded by ancient adversaries and rising from the ashes of eternity, she calls forth three daughters, chief among them the True Lilith, forging a new cosmic equilibrium that forever rewrites the laws of magic.

“When the dragon awakens within divine bone, balance is no longer asked for—it is commanded.”
Reader & Distribution Note

Volume II expands the mythic scope to cosmic dragon transformations and the creation of the True Lilith. Inquire directly for details and reader materials.
