A destiny written beyond the boundaries of heaven and earth.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Meet Billie Grace Berry

The Mind Behind the Mythology

“Billie Grace Berry invites readers into a world where ancient mythology meets extraordinary imagination. Through stories of celestial powers, dragons, magic, and the enduring legend of Lilith, her work explores transformation, identity, destiny, and the boundaries between worlds.”

Imbued with a love for deep lore, cosmic hierarchies, and high-stakes supernatural drama, Billie Grace Berry weaves intricate tapestries where mortal boundaries dissolve into epic celestial conflicts. Her books chart the paths of complex souls striving for redemption, self-determination, and the restoration of eternal equilibrium.
